# 🎮 GTA 6 Spoiler Shield

**Protect yourself from Grand Theft Auto VI spoilers across the internet.**

GTA 6 Spoiler Shield is a custom filter collection designed for Safari users running **uBlock Origin Lite** on macOS, iPhone and iPad.

Its purpose is simple: help you avoid GTA 6 spoilers until you are ready to experience the game yourself.

## 🛡️ What does it block?

The filter collection aims to hide GTA 6-related content across supported websites.

### YouTube
- GTA 6 search results
- Videos mentioning GTA 6
- GTA 6 recommendations
- Shorts and playlists
- Spoiler-related video titles
- Comments mentioning GTA 6 characters and story details

### Google Search
- GTA 6-related search results
- Articles and links mentioning GTA 6
- Results mentioning known characters and locations

### Reddit
- Posts mentioning GTA 6
- GTA 6-related discussions
- Posts containing character names and story references

### General websites
- GTA 6 articles
- Story spoilers
- Ending explanations
- Mission walkthroughs
- Plot twists
- Character references
- Known locations and story details

## 📥 Installation

### Requirements

- Safari on macOS, iOS or iPadOS
- uBlock Origin Lite installed and enabled
- Support for custom filtering in your installed extension version

### Step 1 – Install uBlock Origin Lite

Install uBlock Origin Lite from the official Apple App Store:

https://apps.apple.com/app/ublock-origin-lite/id6745342698

### Step 2 – Download the filters

Download the `custom-filters.txt` file from this repository.

### Step 3 – Add the filters

1. Open uBlock Origin Lite settings.
2. Find the custom filtering section.
3. Open or import the contents of `custom-filters.txt`, depending on the available options.
4. Save your changes.
5. Make sure Safari allows the extension to filter content on the websites you visit.

### Step 4 – Test the filters

Try opening this YouTube search:

https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=gta+6

GTA 6-related results should be hidden where the supported filters are applied.

If the filters do not work, check whether your version of uBlock Origin Lite supports the filter syntax being used.

## ⚠️ Important limitations

GTA 6 Spoiler Shield is designed to reduce accidental exposure to spoilers, not guarantee complete protection.

Please keep the following limitations in mind:

- Some cosmetic filters may not work in every version of uBlock Origin Lite.
- YouTube can dynamically load content that may not be detected.
- Images and thumbnails without recognizable text may still appear.
- Spoilers using unknown code names or indirect references may bypass the filters.
- Filters may occasionally hide unrelated content containing matching keywords.
- Custom filter support can differ between macOS and iOS/iPadOS.

**No spoiler filter can guarantee 100% protection.** Use caution when browsing gaming communities, social media and video platforms.
